pudding herself and says, "He'd only want me to enjoy it twice." Dad
pretends not to cry into the potatoes, and I pretend not to notice.

Warm, funny and heartfelt: a small Sunday ritual, a wry line from
Grandma, and a father quietly moved — a whole household in two lines.
